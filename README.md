I live in Hong Kong and build AI that has to survive contact with real work. Most of what I publish is an **open agent skill**: you install it in a personal agent, and it turns a messy input — a talking-head recording, a meeting, a job hunt — into something you can review, approve, and actually use.

## Open agent skills

### [DraftCut](https://github.com/PoetCoderJun/DraftCut)

Speak first. Keep your own voice. Your agent tightens the rambling, then builds a concise, designed talking video — subtitles, diagrams, and pacing included.

### [DingTalk-style Minutes](https://github.com/PoetCoderJun/dingtalk-style-minutes)

A phone memo, a meeting, or an interview becomes DingTalk-闪记-style graphic minutes on Feishu: an overview, evidence-linked notes, and a full timestamped transcript.

### [linkedin-apply-for-agents](https://github.com/PoetCoderJun/linkedin-apply-for-agents)

LinkedIn auto-apply as a skill for personal agents — Grok Bot, OpenAI Dots, Claude, Codex, and similar runtimes. Name a location first; if it is Hong Kong, JobsDB is included automatically.

## Also online

[PoetCut](https://poetcut.online/) is my AI talking-video editor for Chinese creators: upload a raw recording, cut the filler, and export a subtitled, chaptered video you can publish.

## How I like to work

I care less about a one-shot demo, and more about a workflow you can inspect: a plan you can approve, evidence you can check, and an artifact you can keep using.
